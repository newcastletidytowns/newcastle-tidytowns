Privacy

Photos & Privacy

Newcastle Tidy Towns is a voluntary community group in Newcastle, Co. Dublin. This note explains how we handle photos and any details you send us. Any questions, email hello@newcastlecodublintidytowns.ie.

Photos at our events and around the village

We take photos at clean-ups, planting days, sales and other activities to show the work of the group and encourage people to get involved. Some of these may show people who are recognisable. We use them on this website and our social media in the ordinary course of promoting the group's work. We try to favour wide or general shots, and we'll always take a photo down on request.

Children

We take extra care with photos of children. We won't identify a child by name, and if you're a parent or guardian and would prefer a photo of your child not to appear, just tell us and we'll remove it.

Asking us to remove a photo

If you appear in a photo and would rather it wasn't online, email us and we'll take it down promptly — no reason needed.

If you contact us

When you email us or send us photos, we use your message only to reply and to run the group's activities. We don't sell it or pass it on, and we keep it no longer than we need to.

Your rights

You can ask to see what we hold about you, have it corrected, or have it removed. Contact us at the email above. If you're not happy with how we've handled things, you can contact the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).
