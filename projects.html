Launching the Bulbs not Bonfire campaign

With the Mayor of South Dublin County Council, 5 October 2026

We were delighted to welcome Honourable Mayor Francis Timmons of South Dublin County Council to Newcastle to launch the Bulbs not Bonfire campaign with the Newcastle Co. Dublin Tidy Towns team. The idea is a simple one: brighten up the village with spring bulbs rather than marking Halloween with bonfires. Bags of bulbs were handed out on the day for planting around the area, so there will be plenty of colour to look forward to next spring.

A big thank you to the Mayor and to South Dublin County Council for their support, and to everyone who came along on the day. You can read more about the Bulbs not Bonfire launch and other events on the South Dublin County Council website.

A helping hand from Microsoft

Volunteers paint the wall at the play space, October 2026

The Bric-a-Brac Sale

A great turnout at St Finian's Community Hall, September 2026

A big thank you from Newcastle Tidy Towns

We would like to thank all those who donated items for our recent Bric-a-Brac sale. We got a huge collection of goods and a wonderful variety to suit young and not so young. It was lovely to see people leaving with a smile, happy that they had got a bargain. We kept the prices low, as all the items were donated.

Thank you to Karen and Brian Kelly for donating the bags, which were most useful. Thank you also to the many volunteers who gave up their time to help out on Saturday and Sunday. We plan to make a donation to the local Youth Club, who helped out on the day.

A huge thank you to Gerard Cullen and the Newcastle Development (Hall Committee) Trust for letting us use the hall and, most of all, for donating the money to Newcastle Tidy Towns.

Finally, thank you to all the residents who supported us on Sunday and came out in such huge numbers. It was all very much appreciated.
