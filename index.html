Newcastle · South Dublin

Looking after the place we call home.

We're a group of neighbours keeping Newcastle village clean, green and welcoming — through clean-ups, planting days, biodiversity work and a bit of heritage care. Everyone's welcome, no experience needed.

What we do

Small, regular efforts add up. Here's where our energy goes across the year.

Clean streets & approaches

Regular litter picks along the main street, the church grounds and the roads into the village, so Newcastle looks its best all year round.

Planting & floral displays

Seasonal planting, tubs and beds around the village — brightening the streetscape and keeping green spaces cared for.

Biodiversity & pollinators

Wildflower areas, pollinator-friendly planting and reducing mowing where it helps nature — supporting the All-Ireland Pollinator Plan.

Heritage & sense of place

Caring for the features that make Newcastle special, from St Finian's and the historic village core to signage and shared spaces.
