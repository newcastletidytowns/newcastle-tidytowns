Looking after the place we call home.

We're a group of local people who give a bit of time to keep Newcastle village tidy and green. We do litter picks, planting, and small jobs around the place. Anyone can join in, and you don't need to know anything beforehand.

What we do

A few hours here and there really does add up. This is what we get up to during the year.

Litter picks

We go around the main street, the church grounds and the roads into the village and pick up the litter, so the place stays looking well.

Planting & flowers

We plant and look after the tubs, beds and flowers around the village to brighten the place up a bit.

Helping nature

We grow wildflowers, plant things the bees and butterflies like, and leave some areas to grow wild. It's part of the All-Ireland Pollinator Plan.

Looking after the village

We keep an eye on the things that make Newcastle what it is, from St Finian's and the old village centre to the signs and shared spaces.
